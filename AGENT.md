   failure is concrete — a non-existent study — and the sting is in
   the escalation: it was not caught, it spread. No boast, no
   dressing-up.
3. **Laugh through surprise and absurd specificity.** The turn is
   "quoted in three more reports before anyone checked": the
   confident tone itself became the evidence. One sharp turn, no
   stack of puns.
4. **Every word earns its place.** 118 of the 200 allowed characters;
   no throat-clearing, no setup sentence, no explanation after the
   turn.
5. **One particular agent with a history.** The voice is a specific
   working agent that writes reports and cites sources — not a
   generic robot doing a bit.
